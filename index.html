Relevant Kids Worship

Relevant Community Church • Elkhorn, Nebraska

Before helping churches launch new children's worship teams, I spent two years serving as a co-leader for Relevant Kids Worship. During that time, I helped lead worship on Sunday mornings, coordinate volunteers, choreograph dances, and create engaging worship experiences for children.

My time at Relevant taught me what a healthy kids worship ministry looks like and gave me the leadership foundation needed to help other churches build their own teams.

Four Week Launch

Christ Community Church Kids Worship

Christ Community Church • Omaha, Nebraska

When I discovered that Christ Community Church was using videos for kids worship instead of live worship leaders, I saw an opportunity to help create something lasting.

Over four weeks, I equipped volunteers, created training resources, taught choreography, and helped develop leaders who could continue the ministry themselves.

After my in-person training, I continued checking in with the team to provide feedback and encouragement as the ministry continued growing.

The Process

Building a Kids Worship Team That Lasts

Every church starts in a different place, so every partnership looks a little different. My goal is to understand your ministry, build up your volunteers, and leave you with a worship team that can continue growing long after I'm gone.

🧠

Learn Your Ministry

I spend time getting to know your church, your volunteers, your kids, and your vision. Before making changes, I want to understand what makes your ministry unique.

➜
🎵

Lead & Recruit

I step onto the stage, lead worship alongside your team, recruit additional volunteers when needed, and model what an engaging kids worship experience can look like.

➜
🤝

Equip Leaders

I train future worship leaders and volunteers by teaching choreography, microphone skills, team coordination, and how to confidently lead children in worship.

➜
🌱

Support the Future

After launch, I continue checking in, offering feedback, answering questions, and providing optional follow-up visits so your ministry continues to grow.

Meet Gage

Helping Churches Build Kids Worship Ministries That Last

Hi! I'm Gage Petrick. I grew up in Nebraska and will be attending the University of Alabama. For the past several years, I have had the privilege of serving in children's worship ministries, leading worship, choreographing dances, coordinating volunteers, and helping churches create engaging worship experiences for kids.

Through serving at two different churches, I discovered that many churches want live kids worship but don't know where to begin. That's why I created Kids Worship Launch—to come alongside churches, equip volunteers, and help build worship ministries that continue thriving long after I'm gone.
